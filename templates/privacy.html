Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information (email and password)
  • Financial data (expenses, categories, and amounts you log)
  • Preferences for tracking and reporting

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely to provide and improve the Spendly service. This includes:

  • Maintaining your user account
  • Generating your spending summaries and visualizations
  • Providing customer support and technical updates

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard encryption to protect your financial records from unauthorized access. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or trade your personal or financial data to third parties. We may use trusted third-party service providers to help us operate our website (e.g., hosting providers), but they are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
